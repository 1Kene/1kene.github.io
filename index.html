How I work a deal

The product changes from one sector to the next. The method does not.

  1. Discover

    Ask before pitching. What does the problem cost per month, and who feels it?

  2. Quantify

    Turn the offer into payback, return and risk, in the client's own numbers.

  3. Translate

    Explain the technology so a finance lead and an engineer both say yes.

  4. Negotiate

    Bring every party costed options, so the talk is about choices and not positions.

  5. Deliver

    Stay through installation and handover. The next sale starts with this one working.

Selected work

Three projects from my time at EMONE Energy Inc. (formerly EM-ONE Energy Solutions) and Energy Innovation Capital. Each one shows a skill that carries to any technical product.

Keeping an award-winning plant earning its keep

Account care and reporting

Problem
A federal ministry depended on a 1.52MW solar and 2.28MWh battery plant, and its leaders needed proof it was performing.
What I did
Took day-to-day responsibility for the plant, set up supplier escalation and spares planning, and wrote weekly and monthly reports that decision-makers could act on.
Result
90% availability over 18 months, repair delays down 30% and downtime down 25%. The plant was named Best C&I Solar Project of the Year at the Africa Energy Forum.

Breaking a deadlock in the middle of construction

Negotiation and stakeholder alignment

Problem
A 600kWp microgrid build stalled when site access and equipment logistics pulled the client, contractors and international suppliers in different directions.
What I did
Brought the parties together around three costed options, so everyone could see what each path would cost in time and money.
Result
Delivered ahead of schedule with zero recordable safety incidents and a 70% cut in diesel dependency.

Judging 15+ ventures on their numbers

Commercial analysis

Problem
A venture capital firm needed to compare early-stage energy companies across sub-Saharan Africa on a like-for-like basis.
What I did
Built NPV and cash-flow models for each venture and set out the cost, return and risk side by side.
Result
15+ ventures assessed with one consistent method, the same one I now use to build a client's business case.
